Create **distinctive, production-grade web interfaces** that avoid generic AI aesthetics.

## Core Approach

Understand context first: "What problem does this interface solve? Who uses it?" Commit to a **bold direction** — brutally minimal, maximalist, retro-futuristic, or another distinctive tone — then execute with precision.

## Key Design Principles

**Typography matters**: Choose "fonts that are beautiful, unique, and interesting" rather than defaults. Pair distinctive display fonts with refined body fonts.

**Aesthetic commitment**: "Bold maximalism and refined minimalism both work — the key is intentionality, not intensity." Color palettes should be cohesive with dominant colors and sharp accents.

**Spatial creativity**: Encourage asymmetry, overlap, diagonal flow, and grid-breaking elements rather than predictable layouts.

## What to Avoid

- Generic typefaces (Arial, Inter, Roboto)
- Clichéd color schemes and overused patterns
- Cookie-cutter design that lacks context-specific character
- Common defaults across projects
- Excessive centered layouts, purple gradients, uniform rounded corners

## Philosophy

Match implementation complexity to your vision. Maximalist designs warrant elaborate code; minimalist ones demand restraint and precision. Intentional execution creates memorable interfaces.
